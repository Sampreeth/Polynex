About Us

Home > About Us

Polynex was established In the year 1982 in Bangalore, India. Over the years we have gained immense knowledge in our field of concern and have successfully met the expectations of our esteemed customers.

Our clientele over these years have been Hindustan Aeronautics Limited(India), Seabird Aviation (Jordan), Boeing 787 Project, Plexion, Gas Turbine Research for Defence Lab, NAVY, and DRDO.

We have manufactured assembly jigs for Light Combat Aircraft (LCA) for HAL which in turn is supplied to the Indian Air Force. We have supplied Wind Shield for SARAS aircraft for NAL, jointly supplied by Pilkington aerospace (GKN AEROSPACE, UK) and Polynex.

We have also manufacture alieron actuator Test rig for Boeing for the 787.

We are an AS ( Aviation standards) compliant company
